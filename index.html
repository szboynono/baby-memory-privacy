BABY MEMORY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Baby Memory is a private care log for parents and caregivers. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your iPhone, and the choices you have.

1. Your log stays on your iPhone

Baby Memory v1.0 does not create an account. Your baby profile, care events, edits, supplements, settings, and reminder schedule are stored locally on your iPhone. v1.0 does not sync your log to a server or to another device.

Deleting the app removes the app’s local data from that iPhone, subject to iOS backups you control. There is no Baby Memory account to delete.

Short version: the log is local. Audio is processed on the device. When you use Talk or Ask online, the text you submit is sent through our proxy to an AI provider to produce the result. We do not use it for advertising.

2. Voice and Talk

Voice capture and speech recognition run on your iPhone. Baby Memory does not store your audio and does not send audio files to us or to an AI provider.

When you use Talk while online, the transcribed sentence is sent through our proxy to DeepSeek to turn it into a structured log entry. Before the first Talk, the app asks for your permission and explains this. If you decline, you can still log manually with the buttons.

3. Ask

When you use Ask, your question and the day’s relevant logged events are sent through our proxy to DeepSeek to generate the answer. This may include caregiver names in your baby space. The app asks for your permission before the first Ask. Answers are for general information and are not medical advice.

4. Our proxy and AI provider

Requests pass through a Cloudflare Worker that we operate. The proxy is designed not to retain the content of your question, transcript, or answer. It keeps coarse counters for rate limits and records needed to verify App Attest device requests.

DeepSeek processes the request to produce the response and may retain data under its own API terms. We do not control DeepSeek’s retention. We do not use your content for advertising or sell it.

5. What we do not collect

  • No account, email, phone number, or password.
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or third-party tracking SDK.
  • No sale of personal information.

6. Purchases

Baby Memory v1.0 is free and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

7. Children

Baby Memory is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children. The app is not directed to children under 13. A parent or caregiver chooses what information about a child to enter.

8. Security

Local data is protected by iOS file protection. Network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its App Store listing where required.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: github.com/szboynono.
